Privacy

Last updated 2 September 2026

The short version

Your files and messages go straight from one device to the other. They are not uploaded and not stored, and the server's only job is to introduce the two devices to each other — it is deliberately built so that it could not read your files even if it wanted to. When your two networks will not let the devices talk directly, an encrypted relay passes the data along without being able to read it either.

There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

What the server handles

  • The 4-digit code and a random session key, held only while the session is alive. A code stops being joinable after 60 seconds or as soon as one device uses it.
  • Connection setup messages (the technical description of how the two browsers should reach each other) passed between the two devices and not kept.
  • Your IP address, used to count join attempts for one minute so that nobody can guess their way into someone else's code. It is not written down and the counter is discarded automatically.
  • One anonymous number per session: whether a direct connection worked, and whether it was made over a local network or through a firewall. There is no address, no timestamp beyond the date, and nothing that ties it to a person or a session. The running totals are public at /api/stats.

Session records are deleted when the session ends, five minutes after both devices disappear, or after an hour of silence — whichever comes first.

What the server never receives

  • The contents of your files.
  • The names, sizes or types of your files.
  • The text you send through the message box.

These travel over an end-to-end encrypted connection between the two browsers. When a direct route cannot be opened — mobile carriers often block one — the encrypted stream is carried by Cloudflare's TURN relay instead. The relay forwards ciphertext; the keys stay in the two browsers, so it cannot read what passes through it.

What stays on your device

  • The session code and key, in your browser's per-tab storage, so a reload or a phone locking its screen does not lose the session. Closing the tab clears it.
  • Received files. If you chose a folder when you joined, they are written straight into that folder as they arrive, and this page can reach nothing else on your disk. If you did not, they are held in private browser storage until you save them; unclaimed files there are cleaned up after a day, and clearing your browser's site data removes them immediately.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts this site and the signalling server, and sees the ordinary request information any web host sees.
  • STUN servers run by Cloudflare and Google are asked, during connection setup, how your device appears from the outside. This tells them your IP address; no file data passes through them. When no direct route exists, Cloudflare's TURN relay carries the encrypted stream — it sees your IP address and the amount of traffic, never the content.

Checking any of this

The whole thing is open source under the MIT licence at github.com/michaelcheung0125-svg/GSend. The server is a single small file, and you are welcome to read it rather than take this page's word for it.

隱私說明

最後更新:2026 年 9 月 2 日

簡短版本

你的檔案和訊息是從一台裝置直接傳到另一台,不會上傳、不被儲存。 伺服器唯一的工作是讓兩台裝置認識彼此,而且它的設計讓它就算想看也讀不到你的檔案。 當雙方的網路不允許裝置直接連通時,會由加密中繼轉送,中繼同樣讀不到內容。

沒有帳號、沒有 cookie、沒有分析工具、沒有第三方追蹤。

伺服器會經手什麼

  • 4 位數字碼與一組隨機 session 金鑰,只在連線存續期間保留。 數字碼在 60 秒後、或一有裝置使用後就無法再被加入。
  • 連線建立訊息(描述兩個瀏覽器如何找到彼此的技術資訊),轉交後不保留。
  • 你的 IP 位址,用來在一分鐘內計算加入次數,防止有人靠猜測闖入別人的數字碼。 它不會被寫下來,計數器會自動丟棄。
  • 每次連線一個匿名數字:直連是否成功、是走區域網路還是穿透防火牆。 不含位址、不含日期以外的時間、沒有任何能對應到個人或特定連線的東西。 累計數字公開於 /api/stats。

連線紀錄會在結束時、雙方離開五分鐘後、或閒置一小時後刪除,以先到者為準。

伺服器永遠收不到什麼

  • 你的檔案內容。
  • 檔案的名稱、大小或類型。
  • 你透過訊息框傳送的文字。

這些都走兩個瀏覽器之間的端對端加密連線。當直連無法建立時(行動網路經常擋掉直連), 加密後的資料流會改由 Cloudflare 的 TURN 中繼伺服器轉送。中繼只經手密文, 金鑰始終在兩個瀏覽器手上,它無法讀取經過它的內容。

留在你裝置上的東西

  • 連線代碼與金鑰,存在瀏覽器的分頁層級儲存空間, 讓重新整理或手機鎖螢幕不會弄丟連線。關閉分頁即清除。
  • 收到的檔案。如果你在加入時選了資料夾,檔案會邊收邊直接寫進那個資料夾, 本頁面碰不到你磁碟上的其他任何地方。如果沒有選,檔案會先放在瀏覽器的私有儲存空間, 在你儲存之前一直保留;未取用的檔案會在一天後清理,清除瀏覽器網站資料則會立即移除。

還有誰參與其中

  • Cloudflare 提供本站與信令伺服器的代管,會看到任何網站主機都會看到的一般請求資訊。
  • Cloudflare 與 Google 的 STUN 伺服器會在建立連線時被詢問你的裝置從外部看起來是什麼樣子, 這會讓它們得知你的 IP 位址;沒有任何檔案資料經過它們。當直連不存在時, Cloudflare 的 TURN 中繼會轉送加密後的資料流——它看得到你的 IP 位址與流量大小,永遠看不到內容。

如何自行查證

整個專案以 MIT 授權開源於 github.com/michaelcheung0125-svg/GSend。 伺服器只有一個很小的檔案,你可以自己讀,不必只聽這一頁怎麼說。
